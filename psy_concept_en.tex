The proposed concept offers a geometric explanation for the phenomena of dark matter and dark energy, a new interpretation of quantum nonlocality, and formulates the foundations of a projective science in which ethics functions as the physics of Symphysis.
		
		The work consists of four main parts and seven supplements. The total volume is approximately 200 standard book pages.

	\subsection{The Author's Conception and Methodology}
	
	This work is not an attempt to describe reality, but an attempt to change the very optics through which we view it.
	
	The author proceeds from the premise that modern science has reached the limits of its descriptive capacity. We have created the Standard Model, General Relativity, and Quantum Mechanics — and discovered that they contradict one another, while their joints gape with «dark» entities introduced to salvage the equations. Dark matter and dark energy are not discoveries, but symptoms of crisis.
	
	The $\Psi$-Theory (Theory of the Multivers) proposed here is a radical step. It postulates: we do not see the essence of things; we see only their \textbf{Rasters} — coarse-grained projections of a multidimensional Original onto our low-dimensional brane. All of physics is the science of projection artifacts, not of fundamental reality.
	
	The author's methodology is neither academic nor artistic. It is \textbf{constructive metaphysics}: a synthesis of mathematical models (L(p)-metrics, the D(r)-function), physical principles (holography, gauge projection), and philosophical intuition refracted through the prism of traditional teachings on consciousness. The result is not a proof, but an \textbf{architectural manifesto of a new ontology}, where Consciousness is not an epiphenomenon of matter, but a fundamental property of the Substrate, and ethics is not morality, but geometry.
	
	The aim of this work is not to convince, but to provide a map and compass for independent movement from Raster to Original.

	\subsubsection{The Single Universal Substrate (Original) / Protoformation}
	
	An infinite-dimensional continuum of Space-Time-Energy-Information, lying at the foundation of all being. Philosophical analogies: Plato's «World of Ideas», Kant's «Thing-in-Itself», the «Absolute» in idealism, Tegmark's mathematical multiverse.

	Protoformation $= A \otimes \Omega$, where:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item A (Alpha) — the aspect of «Pattern / Structure / Relation».
		\item $\Omega$ (Omega) — the aspect of «Potential / Flux / Becoming».
	\end{itemize}
	
	These are two entangled pairs of projections:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item $(I, \Pi)$ — «Static Framework»: Information (logic, structure) and Space (arena, geometry of this logic).
		\item $(E, B)$ — «Dynamic Flux»: Energy (potential for change) and Time (arrow, parameter of realization of this potential).
	\end{itemize}
	
	Scientific parallels:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Connection $(I, \Pi)$: The Holographic Principle, AdS/CFT correspondence — information and space are dual.
		\item Connection $(E, B)$: Canonical conjugacy in quantum mechanics ($\Delta E \Delta t \geq \hbar/2$), the interpretation of antiparticles as motion backward in time.
	\end{itemize}
	
	\subsubsection{Gauge Projection}
	
	A fundamental process/relation that generates observable worlds from the Original through the reduction of dimensionality and degrees of freedom. Any observable universe is a low-dimensional gauge projection of the infinite-dimensional Original. Projection is not a spatial mapping, but a reduction of degrees of freedom and dimensionality.
	
	\subsubsection{Wave of Reality (formerly: Quantized Hypersurface of the Present, QHP)}
	
	A holistic and immutable «moment of the Present» — a concrete 3D+1 (or other dimensionality) realization (projection) of the Original. Physical image: not a «time arrow», but a «graph with nodes», where each node is a complete state of the Universe at moment $t_i$. The edges of the graph reflect relations of «similarity» or «causal possibility» (not causality in the classical sense). Connection with existing theories: analogy with the «sum over histories» in path integrals (Feynman), but in the configuration space of the entire Universe. Connection with the concept of the «Tensor Network» in quantum gravity.

	An elementary observable object (particle, field excitation) as a coarse-grained, pixelated shadow of a multidimensional structure of the Original. The elementary particles of the Standard Model (electrons, quarks) are not fundamental objects, but coarse-grained raster projections of complex multidimensional structures of the Original. Their small set of quantum numbers are the «pixels» accessible to our 3D+1 perception. The true nature of particles is multidimensional and geometrically rich, but in our projection, it is discretized and simplified.

	A parameter that orders the Waves of Reality, devoid of any physical distinction between the «past/future» directions. At the level of the set of QHPs, there is no distinguished direction of «past $\to$ future». There exists only an ordering parameter $\tau$, along which QHPs can be arranged.

	\subsection{Law of Scale-Dependent Dimensionality ($D(r)$-Law)}
	
	The effective dimensionality of space $D$ is a continuous (or piecewise continuous) function of the scale of observation $r$:
	\[
	D = D(r).
	\]
	
	\textbf{Behavior of $D(r)$ at various scales:}
	\begin{itemize}
		\item At $r \sim \ell_{\text{Planck}}$: $D(r) \to \infty$ (or a large number). We approach the fullness of the Original, but our projection yields only the simplest «Rasters».
		\item At $r \sim$ human/planetary scales: $D(r) \approx 3$. The Euclidean physics of Newton-Einstein is reproduced.
		\item At $r \sim$ galactic/cosmological scales: $D(r) < 3$ ($2.7 \dots 1$). Space becomes effectively low-dimensional.
	\end{itemize}
	
	\textbf{Main Corollary — Modification of Gravity:} The Newton/Einstein law is generalized:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Potential $\Phi \sim 1/r^{D(r)-2}$.
		\item For $D=3$: classical law $\sim 1/r$.
		\item For $D \approx 2$: potential $\sim \ln(r)$, force $\sim 1/r$.
		\item For $D \approx 1$: potential $\sim r$, force $\sim \text{const}$.
	\end{itemize}
	
	\textbf{Explanation of Astronomical Anomalies:} The constant rotational velocity of stars at the outskirts of galaxies (rotation curves) naturally follows from $D(r) \to 2$ at large scales. This offers a geometric alternative to the dark matter hypothesis. Similarly, the accelerated expansion of the Universe may be a consequence of the change in $D(r)$ at super-large scales.

Motion toward its center (conventionally, into the microcosm) does not lead to a singularity, but triggers a phase transition — a turning inside-out. In this process, our world becomes the «center» of a new, dual space-time sphere.
	
	\textbf{Geometric Analogy ($L(p)$-transformation):} Like a line segment and a semicircle metrically transitioning into one another as the scale of observation changes. This demonstrates the conformal duality between the «large» and the «small», eliminating the absolute hierarchy of scales.
	
	\subsection{Principle of the Nonzero Derivative ($\Pi$-Principle)}

	\[
	F(0) = 0 \quad \text{(Absolute Rest, Unmanifestedness)},
	\]
	\[
	F'(0) = \pi/2 \quad \text{(Memory of Atman, Impulse toward Manifestation)}.
	\]
	
	The derivative at zero is not equal to zero. This is a fundamental property of the Original: it cannot abide in absolute rest, for it stores within itself the «memory» of its infinite nature.
